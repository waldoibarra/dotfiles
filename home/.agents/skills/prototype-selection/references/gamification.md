# Choose game mechanics for user benefit

**Gamification is a credible experiment candidate, not a guarantee of sales or learning.**
Return to [prototype selection](../SKILL.md) for scope, approval, and execution routing.
Use this reference when considering streaks, quests, rewards, progression, competition, or
immersive hybrid apps.

## What the evidence supports

| Evidence | Supported conclusion | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Duolingo streak experiment](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak/), 2020 | Separating streaks from daily goals increased Day-14 retention by 3.3% relatively, according to the company. | Fewer learners reached daily goals; retention and goal attainment can diverge. |
| [Duolingo Streak Wager](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-streaks-keep-duolingo-learners-committed-to-their-language-goals/), 2017 | The company reports better retention and lesson completion in an A/B experiment. | The package includes a reward; this does not isolate streaks alone or establish learning gains. |
| [Independent Duolingo case study](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0958344019000065), 2019 | Its abstract reports learning gains and a moderate time-spent correlation among nine participants. | Small observational study, not causal evidence for gamification; only the abstract was inspected. |
| [Education meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1253549), 2023 | Its synthesis favors gamification overall, with design and context moderators. | Heterogeneous educational findings do not establish shopping conversion or universal effectiveness. |
| [European Commission Temu proceedings](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-opens-formal-proceedings-against-temu-under-digital-services-act), 2024 | The Commission opened an investigation covering potentially addictive design and game-like rewards. | This announcement is not a final violation finding or evidence of commercial uplift. |

This source set does not establish a Temu-specific causal sales effect from gamification.
It also does not establish the current legal disposition of the announced proceedings.
Commercial success alone cannot separate mechanics from prices, coupons, advertising,
distribution, or audience selection.

## Turn the evidence into an ambitious experiment

Choose a concrete mechanism and user benefit instead of requesting generic points and badges.

| Candidate mechanism | Intended benefit to investigate | Counter-signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forgiving streak or recovery mechanic | Returning to useful practice after interruption | Anxiety, minimum-effort activity, or avoidance after losing progress |
| Quests and meaningful progression | Understanding the next step and noticing actual achievement | Completing rewards without completing the underlying task |
| AI character with generated scenes or voice | Motivation, comprehension, expression, or perceived companionship | Misleading capabilities, dependency, inconsistent character, or excessive generation cost |
| Interactive 3D world with visible consequences | Understanding spatial relationships or effects of choices | Spectacle conceals controls, slows tasks, or excludes users |

These are hypotheses derived from the evidence and design possibilities, not validated product
prescriptions.
Game-like presentation, game mechanics, AI behavior, and real user benefit are separate dimensions.
An app can combine them, but adding all dimensions at once makes attribution harder.

## Observe the right outcome

- Name the useful task and success measure before selecting rewards or visual spectacle.
- Compare a plain version or alternative mechanic when that comparison can answer the question.
- Separate return use, conversion, task quality, and user benefit; do not substitute one for
  another.
- Distinguish a novelty reaction from sustained use and record the observation period.
- Account for prices, paid rewards, advertising, and participant selection when interpreting
  results.
- Inspect opt-out, stopping, recovery, accessibility, media latency, and generation cost where
  relevant.
- Avoid misleading scarcity, hidden reward conditions, coercive continuation, or experiments
  exploiting vulnerable participants.

Choose measurement proportional to the decision; a throwaway experiment does not require
production analytics infrastructure.
Use real participant observations for behavior claims; model personas can rehearse research but
cannot establish demand.
