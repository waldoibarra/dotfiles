# Deliver findings with traceable evidence

**Lead with the answer; make its supporting evidence available without burying consequential
uncertainty.**

Read `~/.agents/references/communication-rules.md` before composing the brief.
Use the owning project's glossary for subject terminology.
The dotfiles glossary owns workflow terminology, not facts learned from analyzed videos.

## Keep the evidence chain

For each significant finding, record:

- The observation, speaker claim, or interpretation being reported.
- Source URL, inspected interval, modality, and relevant local evidence paths.
- Timing precision, including chunk-only or segment-relative citations.
- Supporting and contradictory evidence, plus material uncertainty.
- External corroboration only when actually checked, with its source.

A visible dashboard establishes displayed numbers, not authentic revenue or causal proof.
Confidence labels need reasons; avoid invented numerical confidence scores.
A citation must support the nearby conclusion, not merely concern the same topic.

## Use three reading layers

1. **Answer:** State the useful conclusions and their relevance to the agreed question.
2. **Evidence:** Provide timestamped support, selected visuals, and qualifications.
3. **Supporting material:** Link retained transcripts, frame indexes, coverage records, and
  corroborating sources.

Keep the brief proportional to the task.
Create separate files when their use or lifecycle differs; headings can serve small excerpts.
Do not generate six empty reports to satisfy a template.
Opportunity rankings, walkthroughs, and experiments are conditional on the user's goal.

## Record coverage and provenance

The coverage record names the requested source and interval, then records actual capture,
processing, and inspection.
For each relevant modality, identify reviewed intervals, sampling strategy, gaps, failures, and
pending work.
Record tool/model identity and versions, selected tracks, source offsets, and capture timing where
known.
Preserve exact observations separately from edited summaries.
Record whether timestamps are aligned, chunk-bounded, segment-relative, or estimated.

Source media and project findings stay in the authorized project workspace.
Reusable skill directories and dotfiles are not destinations for task recordings or business
evidence.
A missing Git repository does not authorize initializing one.
Retaining artifacts does not authorize committing, uploading, or publishing them.

## Close or hand off

State what completed, what remains uncertain, and whether capture or analysis is still running.
Preserve enough source evidence to revisit consequential findings within the approved retention
policy.
Do not delete original media merely because a transcript or model-generated summary exists.
