# Analyze video through evidence

**Outcome:** Answer the agreed question with traceable findings and explicit inspection limits.

- **IS:** Goal-directed audiovisual analysis, including bounded capture and segmented near-live
  review.
- **IS NOT:** Transcript-only work; use `audio-transcription`.
  Faithful source preservation belongs to `save-md`, not an analytical reconstruction.

YouTube is the supported acquisition host.
Local segments from approved captures are analysis inputs, not authorization for another source.
A transcript cannot establish visual, motion, musical, or non-speech audio evidence.

## Establish the question and limits

Use settled context before asking questions.
Agree the question, source, relevant modalities, excerpt or duration, output destination, and
resource limits.
For live work, settle waiting, capture, interruption recovery, storage, and retention limits.
Read the acquisition reference before choosing those limits.

Choose evidence for the question, not the video's genre.
Lectures, demonstrations, artistic work, and physical processes need different inspection depth.
Business opportunities and experiments are optional outputs, not universal sections.
A bare URL does not authorize acquisition, a playlist crawl, or indefinite monitoring.

## Execute within the agreement

1. Check source access, local tools, available disk, memory pressure, and model capabilities.
2. Acquire only the approved source and intervals; preserve provenance and original tracks.
3. Transcribe speech when relevant, then inspect other required modalities independently.
4. Investigate meaningful transitions, uncertain passages, and consequential numbers at sufficient
  detail.
5. Separate observations, speaker claims, interpretations, and externally corroborated facts.
6. Deliver the answer with evidence links and coverage limitations; retain artifacts at their
  agreed owner.

Use completed segments for near-live analysis while capture continues independently.
Missing capability blocks claims requiring that capability, not unrelated evidence gathering.
Stop rather than bypass access controls or silently substitute captions for speech recognition.

## Completion

The brief answers the agreed question, with source-linked findings and known gaps beside affected
conclusions.
The coverage record distinguishes captured, processed, and inspected intervals for each relevant
modality.
Partial work is labeled partial; transient failures and unreviewed media remain visible.
For ongoing work, report the capture process, deadline, current state, and how to stop it.
A chat reply does not establish that a background recorder continues running.
